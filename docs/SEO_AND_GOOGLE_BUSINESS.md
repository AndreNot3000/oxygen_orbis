## 3. Schema.org JSON-LD & Rich Snippets

The application includes two validated JSON-LD schema blocks in `index.html`:
1. **`Hotel` Schema:** Highlights address, coordinates, star rating (4.9/5), check-in/check-out times, and amenities (Pool, Mac Foster Nightclub, Train Shuttle, 24/7 Power).
2. **`LodgingBusiness` & `OfferCatalog` Schema:** Indexes all 4 room categories (Deluxe King, Executive Room, Presidential Penthouse, Deluxe Twin) with real-time pricing in Nigerian Naira.

### How to Validate with Google:
1. Navigate to [Google Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results).
2. Enter `https://oxygenorbis.com` or paste the source of `index.html`.
3. Confirm that Google detects valid **Hotel** and **LodgingBusiness** rich snippets without errors.
